# Part V — Delivery

## Status
- Owner: the platform owner
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-14

Part V turns the design into a programme; nothing opens because it is drawn. Chapter 23 asks which people must exist, what each does and which duties one person may never combine. Chapter 24 asks in what order the platform opens, what must start on day one because it takes longest, and what it costs. Chapter 25 starts with what to do first, then walks the [143-row register](../12-open-decisions.md#0-the-register-in-one-screen) and every unresolved point across the five gates, asking which decision waits for whom.
