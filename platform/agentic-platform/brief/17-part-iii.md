Part III presents the platform's [first three tenants](../01-hld.md#13-the-three-agents-on-the-platform) as instances of its rules, not exceptions to them. Chapter 15 asks how a doer can hold Super Admin and still be gated, graded and stopped. Chapter 16 asks how a controller without super admin can check it independently.
